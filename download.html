LATEST RELEASE

MonkeFrames 2.0


Changelog

  • UI overhaul: The UI has been completely revamped in order to make it easier to look at and understand for newer MonkeFrames users.
  • Keyframe Editor: The keyframe editor has gotten some upgrades:
    • Curve Editor: Edit transition speed and position with an easy-to-use editor.
    • Smooth transitioning: Keyframes can now smooth themselves out to ease the transition between them.
  • Replays: Record a lobby and play it back with full keyframe editing support. There are also many ways to view your replays:
    • Replay Studio: A professional editor-like replay viewer that has a full timelime with scrubbing, playback speed and voice volume editing.
    • Replay window: Begin, stop, load and export MonkeFrames replays to share them with your friends.
  • Sources: You can now choose many types of camera settings to use while finding keyframes, including orbiting around players, first/third person, and director (auto) view modes to make it easy to follow players around.
    • Player Selector: Select a player on both replays and live lobbies to follow around with the camera.
  • Object Imports: Import .obj files into Gorilla Tag and use them in your animations.
